## Why Teeth Shift After Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment moves teeth by gently guiding them through the bone and soft tissues surrounding them. After your teeth are repositioned, the bone and periodontal ligaments (the connective tissue holding teeth in place) need time to stabilize around your new smile. This remodeling process can take several months or longer, during which your teeth are prone to drift back toward their previous positions — a phenomenon known as **orthodontic relapse**.

Even once your bone has fully adjusted, natural forces from eating, talking, and everyday wear can cause slow tooth movement over time. That’s why retention isn’t just a temporary phase — it’s a lifelong commitment to protecting your investment in a radiant smile.

## Retainer Options Tailored for You

At Platte River Orthodontics, we design retention plans that fit each patient’s unique needs, lifestyle, and treatment history. Here are the most common retainer types we recommend:

- Clear Removable Retainers: These sleek, transparent retainers fit snugly over your teeth and are easy to remove for eating and cleaning. Often called Essix retainers, they offer a discreet way to maintain your smile. Typically, you’ll wear them full-time at first, then transition to nighttime wear as your bite stabilizes.

- Fixed (Bonded) Retainers: A thin wire bonded behind your front teeth provides constant retention without needing you to remember to wear it. Fixed retainers are especially beneficial for those who’ve had significant tooth movement or are at higher risk for relapse.

## The Importance of Wearing Your Retainer

One of the biggest pitfalls we see is patients neglecting their retainers after treatment ends. Even short lapses in retainer use — especially within the first year — can lead to noticeable shifting. If relapse becomes severe, it may require additional orthodontic work, which none of us want after all the effort you’ve put in.

Think of your retainer as an essential part of your oral health routine, just like brushing and flossing. Protecting your smile is a lifelong journey, and we’re here to guide you every step of the way. If you ever need a retainer replacement or adjustments, just give us a call.
