## Invisalign vs. Braces: Finding Your Perfect Smile Solution in Gretna, NE

At Platte River Orthodontics, we understand that every smile we craft is as unique as the person behind it. Whether you’re a teen, adult, or somewhere in between, choosing between Invisalign® clear aligners and traditional braces is a crucial step in your orthodontic journey. With precision and care, Dr. Chad Carter and our team are here to guide you through this decision, supporting your success at every stage of life.

Let’s walk through a practical comparison of Invisalign and braces to help you make an informed choice that fits your lifestyle, goals, and smile aspirations.

### What Are Invisalign® Clear Aligners?

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions. These aligners are removable, making them a popular option for adults and teens seeking discreet orthodontic care. At Platte River Orthodontics, we create your Invisalign plan with state-of-the-art digital scanning and 3D imaging, ensuring a precise fit and efficient treatment timeline.

### Traditional Braces: Still a Powerful Solution

Braces have been a trusted orthodontic method for decades. At our Gretna practice, we offer fully custom braces designed to suit your unique dental anatomy. For younger patients, we also provide Wild Smiles® braces, which add a fun, personalized touch to the orthodontic experience. Braces are fixed appliances, meaning they remain bonded to your teeth throughout treatment, providing consistent and predictable movement.

### Invisalign vs. Braces: A Side-by-Side Comparison

| Feature | Invisalign clear aligners | Traditional braces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appearance | Nearly invisible, clear plastic | Metal or ceramic brackets, including Wild Smiles colors |
| Removability | Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing | Fixed until treatment ends |
| Comfort | Smooth, with minimal irritation | Brackets and wires can cause some discomfort |
| Treatment time | Often 12–18 months, depending on the case | Often 18–24 months, depending on complexity |
| Oral hygiene | Easier, because the trays come out | Brushing and flossing take more care around brackets |
| Food | No restrictions while trays are out | Avoid hard, sticky, or chewy foods |
| Complex cases | Effective for many cases, with limits | Effective for complex corrections |

### Who Is the Ideal Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is an excellent choice for patients who value discretion and flexibility. If your daily routine involves frequent social or professional interactions, or you enjoy eating without restrictions, Invisalign can be a game-changer. Our team will evaluate your smile’s specific needs to see if Invisalign can achieve your desired results. For more information on Invisalign at Platte River Orthodontics, visit our dedicated [Invisalign page](/invisalign/).

### When Should You Choose Braces?

While Invisalign offers exceptional convenience, braces remain the gold standard for complex orthodontic cases, including significant bite corrections and tooth rotations. They are also ideal for younger patients who benefit from the fixed appliance’s consistent force and control. At Platte River Orthodontics, our fully custom braces and Wild Smiles® options combine effectiveness with a personalized touch, ensuring a positive experience for every patient.
