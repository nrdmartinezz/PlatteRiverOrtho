### Why Retention Matters After Braces

Once your braces are removed, your teeth are more vulnerable to shifting because the surrounding bone and gum tissues need time to stabilize. This phase, called retention, is crucial. Without proper retention, teeth can slowly move back toward their original positions—a process known as relapse. Our goal at Platte River Orthodontics is to make sure your smile remains flawless, just as Dr. Carter envisioned when you first started treatment.

Retention is not a one-size-fits-all phase. We customize each retention plan based on your unique treatment history, lifestyle, and oral habits. Whether you wore Wild Smiles braces designed for younger patients or traditional fully custom braces, retention is an essential step to lasting success.

### Types of Retainers We Recommend

At Platte River Orthodontics, we offer several types of retainers, each tailored to different needs and preferences:

- Fixed Retainers: Thin wires bonded to the backside of your front teeth, providing continuous support without the need to remove them.

- Removable Clear Retainers: Nearly invisible and easy to wear, these are a popular choice following Invisalign® treatment or traditional braces.

- Hawley Retainers: A classic design with a sturdy wire and acrylic base, offering durability and adjustability.

Dr. Carter will help you decide which retainer type fits your lifestyle best, ensuring comfort and compliance. Wearing your retainer as prescribed is the key to keeping your teeth perfectly aligned.

### How Long Will You Need to Wear Your Retainer?

Retention timelines vary between patients, but typically, full-time retainer wear is recommended for the first 6 to 12 months after braces come off. After that initial phase, many patients can switch to nighttime-only wear. In some cases, particularly with complex treatments involving jaw surgery or significant tooth movement, lifelong retainer use at night may be advised to prevent relapse.

### Tips to Care for Your Retainer and Smile

Proper retainer care not only prolongs its life but also promotes oral health. Here are a few essential tips from Platte River Orthodontics:

- Clean your retainer daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or recommended cleaning solutions.

- Avoid hot water, which can warp plastic retainers.

- Store your retainer in its protective case when not in use to prevent damage or loss.

- Continue regular dental checkups and cleanings to monitor your teeth and gums.

- Avoid chewing on your retainer or exposing it to harmful chemicals.

By following these guidelines, you’re investing in the longevity of your smile and the success of your orthodontic treatment.

### What Happens If You Don’t Wear Your Retainer?

Skipping retainer wear even occasionally can lead to unwanted tooth movement. Teeth naturally seek equilibrium, so without retention, they gradually drift, undoing months or years of orthodontic progress. This can result in crooked teeth, gaps reappearing, or bite problems returning.

If you ever feel your teeth shifting, contact Platte River Orthodontics immediately. Early intervention can often prevent the need for retreatment or additional appliances.
