If the thought of visiting the orthodontist makes your heart race, you’re far from alone. Studies reveal that nearly three-quarters of adults experience some degree of dental anxiety, with more than a quarter facing intense fear. At **Platte River Orthodontics**, Dr. Chad Carter understands how this fear can stand in the way of achieving a healthy, radiant smile. Our mission is to transform your orthodontic journey into one defined by comfort, confidence, and control.

## Understanding the Roots of Dental Anxiety

For many adults, dental anxiety traces back to earlier experiences—often from childhood—that left lasting impressions of discomfort, pain, or feeling unheard. Such memories can create a barrier to regular oral care, resulting in a cycle where avoidance leads to worsening dental issues. When problems finally demand attention, treatments might be more involved and intimidating, reinforcing the original anxiety.

Breaking this cycle starts with compassionate care and an environment where your concerns are genuinely heard and addressed. At Platte River Orthodontics, we combine expert clinical care with empathetic communication to make every visit a positive step forward.
