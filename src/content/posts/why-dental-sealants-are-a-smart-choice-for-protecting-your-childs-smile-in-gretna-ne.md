At Platte River Orthodontics, led by Dr. Chad Carter, we believe prevention is the cornerstone of a lifetime of healthy smiles. One of the simplest yet most effective ways to guard your child’s teeth from decay is through dental sealants — a quick, painless treatment that acts as a shield against cavities during the years when kids’ teeth are most vulnerable.

## What Exactly Are Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth (molars and premolars). These teeth have natural grooves and pits where food particles and bacteria love to hide — places that can be tricky for even the most thorough brushing. Sealants fill in those grooves, creating a smooth barrier that keeps decay-causing agents out.

The process is straightforward and comfortable. Dr. Carter and our team will clean and dry the tooth, lightly prepare the surface with a safe etching solution, and then apply the sealant material. Using a special curing light, the sealant hardens quickly, providing immediate protection. Each tooth takes just minutes, and your child won’t feel a thing.

## Strong Science Supports Sealants

Decades of research back the protective power of dental sealants. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), sealants can reduce the risk of cavities in molars by up to 80%. Children without sealants are nearly three times more likely to develop decay compared to those who have them.

Despite their proven benefits, many families haven’t yet incorporated sealants into their child’s dental care routine. At Platte River Orthodontics, we’re committed to sharing this knowledge so families can make the best choices for their children’s oral health.

## When Is the Best Time for Sealants?

Timing is key. Sealants work best when applied as soon as permanent teeth fully erupt, before cavities can take hold. Here’s a general guide to when your child’s teeth are ready for sealants:

In some cases, sealants can also be applied to baby molars for children who are especially prone to cavities, offering extra protection during those early years.

Sealants are durable and monitored at every visit to ensure they’re intact. If needed, we can easily reapply them to maintain your child’s defense against decay. Considering the time, expense, and discomfort involved with treating cavities, sealants are an excellent preventive investment.
